Common upgrades across all patterns

  • 👁 Password visibility toggle — #1 usability improvement per Baymard research
  • ✅ "Remember me" — reduces returning user friction
  • 🍎 Apple Sign-In — required on iOS apps if any social login present
  • 📱 inputmode="email" — triggers correct keyboard on mobile
  • ⚠️ Inline field errors — below field, not top of form
  • ↩️ Auto-focus first field on load
